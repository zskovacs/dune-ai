# Content coverage audit contract

`coverage/inventory.json` is the sole manually authored source for audit quantities and evidence. It is an **offline audit** and is never loaded into engine rulesets or included in canonical game identities. The current inventory contains sources and expectations only; empty `content`, `capabilities`, `interactions`, `batches`, and `configurations` mean **not audited yet**, not complete or supported. The strict Python model and validator accept later authored records through the contract below.

## Root and stable references

The root has `schemaVersion: "dune-content-coverage-v1"`, `auditRevision` (this audit's authored revision), `repositoryRevision` (the Git baseline assessed), and the seven named collections. `src-*` and `exp-*` IDs are stable, globally unique within their namespaces. Later records refer to these IDs; renaming one requires a deliberate migration of references. `auditRevision` does not change an engine fingerprint, content version, ruleset revision, or replay format. Keep these collections in deterministic ID order when editing. The current data has a source-first, then product/scope order; a later formatter may normalize that order without changing IDs.

## Source records

Each `source` has `id`, `kind`, `title`, exact `locator`, `revision`, nullable `sha256` and `pages`, `products`, `claimTypes`, `completeness`, `verification`, `rights`, and `notes`. `src-I`, `src-A`, `src-X`, `src-M`, `src-U`, `src-S`, and `src-B` refer to the seven exact local files and hashes in [the rules index](../rules-index.md). PDF pages and evidence pages are **one-based PDF pages**. A file hash identifies bytes, not correctness of every face. Web revisions are observation dates unless an immutable commit is given. Community identity discovery, quantity, printed face semantics, module applicability, and official rulings are distinct claims. `rights` is an audit finding about permission evidence; it grants no reuse. No source record implies complete inventory or automated import suitability.

## Inventory expectations

An expectation contains `id`, `product`, `edition`, `category`, nullable `tier`, `expectedCopies`, nullable `uniqueDefinitions`, `unit`, `basis`, `scope`, at least one `evidence` locator (`sourceId`, one-based `page`, `locator`), and nullable `note`. Product values are exactly `Imperium`, `Uprising`, `RiseOfIx`, `Immortality`, and `Bloodlines`. Categories use current `ContentCategory` names in `ContentDefinition.cs`; `GameplayComponent` is an **audit-only** category for pieces/tokens outside the runtime catalog enum. It does not register an engine definition. `expectedCopies` is a nonnegative official quantity for the stated basis and scope. `uniqueDefinitions` is `null` when a component total gives no unique-face total; never fill it from a community row count. Known distinct starter identities, three Reserve identities, eight physical Leader cards, and 22 printed board locations are stated only where the source establishes them.

`scope` separates boxed pools, boxed tier/identity subsets, ordinary per-player recipes, standard setup components, and excluded solo/two-player, Epic, six-player, CHOAM, Tech, or leader-specific material. A subset **overlaps** its parent boxed pool and must not be summed into it. The 18 original Conflicts split 4 + 10 + 4, while ordinary setup selects 1 + 5 + 4 = 10; the latter is not the box total. Original Reserve cards split 8 + 10 + 6 = 24. Original starter copies are four ten-card player sets = 40; each set has seven identities and 2 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 10. Three-player games use only 30 starter copies. The original board's 22 locations are named spaces, not loose cards. Alternative wood/plastic Bloodlines Commanders form a seven-unit usable pool, not fourteen usable units. Uprising likewise has four boxed ten-card starter sets and a distinct ten-card per-player recipe (U p.3). Ix has four boxed Control the Spice cards but only one per player in excluded Epic mode (X p.2); Immortality has eight boxed Experimentation cards but replaces two starters per player (M pp.3,5).

Do not turn a count into a face, effect, eligibility predicate, ability, or readiness claim. Module-scoped totals remain physically boxed totals even when the selected configuration excludes them. Keep unclear edition effects, print differences, and rights disputes as explicit limitations. A future complete pool requires identity-level, quantity-level, applicability-level, and face-level evidence separately.

## Updating restricted local evidence

Acquire source files personally from the publisher or a legitimately held copy. Keep PDFs, scans, extracted text, images, spreadsheets, and scraped data in ignored local storage; do not commit them or fetched website bodies. Record the exact edition/URL or owner-held provenance, retrieval date, SHA-256 (`sha256sum <file>`), page bounds (`pdfinfo <file>`), and one-based page/section for each claim. Compare the new hash with the pinned source record. Inspect relevant page renders for symbols, tiers, layout and obscured faces; text extraction is only a search aid. Manually enter minimal, independently reviewed metadata and cite the claim. Check official FAQ/errata and module exclusions, note disagreements without choosing a convenient count, and review reuse rights before copying any text or art. Validation and generated reports must run offline, and missing optional local copyrighted files must be reported as unavailable evidence without fetching them automatically.

## S2 typed authoring contract

The validator lives in `scripts/content_coverage_model.py`; `scripts/content_coverage.py validate` checks the authored inventory offline. The seven root arrays remain `sources`, `expectations`, `content`, `capabilities`, `interactions`, `batches`, and `configurations`. Abilities are nested in their owning `content` record. All record keys and enum values are exact and case-sensitive; unknown keys, missing required keys, booleans in integer fields, and unknown products/categories/slots fail loading. Nullable quantities distinguish unknown from zero. Empty collections and incomplete faces are never complete coverage.

| Record | Required fields beyond `id` | Optional fields with defaults |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source | `kind`, `title`, `locator`, `revision`, `sha256`, `pages`, `products`, `claimTypes`, `completeness`, `verification`, `rights`, `notes`, `discrepancies` | `localPath: null`, `reusePermission: false` |
| Expectation | `product`, `edition`, `category`, `tier`, `expectedCopies`, `uniqueDefinitions`, `unit`, `basis`, `scope`, `evidence`, `note` | none |
| Content | `name`, `product`, `edition`, `category`, `version`, `variantOf`, `authenticity`, `boxedQuantity`, `perPlayerQuantity`, `expectationIds`, `evidence`, `discovery`, `sourcing`, `verification`, `faceComplete`, `slots`, `abilities`, `availability` | `faceEvidence: []`, `runtimeDefinition: false`, `replaces: []`, `excludes: []` |
| Slot | `slot`, `discovery` | `reason: null`, `evidence: []`, `abilityIds: []` |
| Ability | `id`, `contentId`, `slot`, `timing`, `classification`, `semanticClaim`, `descriptors`, `evidence`, `capabilityIds`, `interactionIds`, `implementationRefs`, `testRefs`, `discovery`, `sourcing`, `verification`, `implementation`, `validation`, `blockers`, `exclusionReason`, `batchId` | `classificationReason: null` |
| Capability | `contract`, `supportedSubset`, `memberRefs`, `testRefs`, `missingDelta`, `prerequisiteIds`, `implementation`, `validation`, `blockers` | none |
| Interaction | `participantIds`, `rulesets`, `evidence`, `exceptions`, `requiredHook`, `implementationRefs`, `testRefs`, `implementation`, `validation`, `blockers` | none |
| Batch | `memberIds`, `prerequisiteIds`, `sourceGates`, `mechanics`, `expectedDelta`, `acceptance`, `risks` | none |
| Configuration | `base`, `expansions`, `modules`, `mode`, `seats`, `leaderPolicy`, `authenticity`, `contentIds`, `interactionIds`, `evidence`, `initializationTests`, `fullGameTests`, `rlTests` | none |

`availability` is a required typed object with `baseGames`, `requiredExpansions`, `excludedExpansions`, `requiredModules`, `excludedModules`, `modes`, `minimumSeats`, and `maximumSeats`. Base values are `Imperium`/`Uprising`; expansions are `RiseOfIx`/`Immortality`/`Bloodlines`; modules are `UprisingChoam`/`BloodlinesTech`; modes are `Standard`/`Epic`. Empty allowlists mean any. Seat bounds must be ordered within 1–6, and required/excluded sets cannot conflict. The configuration's `mode` and `modules` use these same enums. A selected configuration must include every applicable, reachable definition linked to its expectations, and cannot select an unavailable one. Solo/two-player, six-player, Epic, CHOAM, Tech, leader-specific, and original-versus-Uprising scopes are evaluated for the configuration before readiness; excluded scopes do not impose permanent gaps on ordinary 3/4-seat games.

Evidence claims use `sourceId`, nullable `page`, and `locator`, with optional `kind` and `verification`. Local PDF claims need a one-based page within the pinned source bound. A verified discovered content identity needs an independently verified official `identity` claim; verified content copies need an independently verified official `quantity` claim. Verified ability semantics require a `semantics` claim. `faceComplete: true` additionally needs `faceEvidence` containing a verified official whole-face `semantics` claim and a complete category-specific slot account. Every potentially relevant slot is `discovered` with an ability ID or `verified-absent` with its own verified official semantics claim. The validator's category slot map is deliberately conservative; extend it through schema review when a new timing is found. Empty ability arrays are valid only when the relevant slots are explicitly verified absent. Source PDF bytes are checked against pinned hashes if present; missing local files produce warnings without a download. `reusePermission: true` requires the explicit rights value `licensed-for-copied-artifacts`; unknown or restrictive findings grant no copying permission.

A descriptor is `{ "kind": "precondition|cost|choice|role|usage|effect", "value": "concise description" }`. A blocker is `{ "id": "...", "open": true|false, "blocking": true|false, "scope": "...", "reason": "..." }`. A test reference is `{ "path": "repo-relative path", "name": "test name", "kind": "behavior|integration|privacy-replay|inventory|initialization|full-game|rl|...", "historical": false }`. A historical or `inventory` reference cannot satisfy `tested`. Implementation references are repository-relative `path::member` strings. `authenticity` is `official` or `synthetic`; `GameplayComponent` remains audit-only and cannot assert `runtimeDefinition`.

The independent enums are discovery (`not-discovered`, `discovered`), sourcing (`unsourced`, `partially-sourced`, `fully-sourced`), verification (`unverified`, `verified`, `disputed`), classification (`primitive`, `composite`, `special`, `unresolved`), implementation (`not-implemented`, `partially-implemented`, `implemented`, `unsupported`), and validation (`not-tested`, `tested`). Slot discovery is `undiscovered`, `unreadable`, `discovered`, or `verified-absent`; the latter requires face evidence and owns no ability. Slots are `Setup`, `Passive`, `Agent`, `Reveal`, `Acquire`, `SignetRing`, `Intrigue`, `Combat`, `PostWin`, `Endgame`, `Triggered`, and `PersistentModifier`. Timings are `setup`, `passive`, `on-placement`, `on-reveal`, `on-acquire`, `on-signet-ring`, `intrigue`, `combat`, `post-win`, `endgame`, `triggered`, and `persistent`. A nested ability ID has form `contentId#slot:key`, and its matching slot lists that ID in `abilityIds`.

`basis` and `scope` are strict enums covering the authored S1 values; adding a new physical basis or applicability scope requires a reviewed schema extension. `build_report` computes one quantity result per expectation: verified copies, sourced but unverified copies, unknown-copy record count, missing residual copies (null when any copy count is unknown), overcount, known unique total (nullable), discovered definitions, and completeness. Known overcounts and excess definitions are integrity errors. Missing residuals and unknown totals are explicit valid gaps. Configuration readiness fails closed on missing composition evidence or inventory, omitted reachable definitions, unsupported abilities or required interactions, missing authentic initializer/full-game/RL behavior evidence, or synthetic content. Capability prerequisites must be supported transitively. Fully supported abilities require current behavioral, integration, and privacy/replay test references.
