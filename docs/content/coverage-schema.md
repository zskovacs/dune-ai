# Content coverage audit contract

`coverage/inventory.json` is the sole manually authored source for audit quantities and evidence. It is an **offline audit** and is never loaded into engine rulesets or included in canonical game identities. Authored content, capabilities, interactions, batches and configurations retain incomplete and unsupported findings explicitly. An empty collection means **not audited yet**, not complete or supported. The strict Python model and validator enforce the contract below.

## Root and stable references

The root has `schemaVersion: "dune-content-coverage-v1"`, `auditRevision` (this audit's authored revision), `repositoryRevision` (the Git baseline assessed), and the seven named collections. `src-*` and `exp-*` IDs are stable, globally unique within their namespaces. Later records refer to these IDs; renaming one requires a deliberate migration of references. `auditRevision` does not change an engine fingerprint, content version, ruleset revision, or replay format. Keep these collections in deterministic ID order when editing. The current data has a source-first, then product/scope order; a later formatter may normalize that order without changing IDs.

## Source records

Each `source` has `id`, `kind`, `title`, exact `locator`, `revision`, nullable `sha256` and `pages`, `products`, `claimTypes`, `completeness`, `verification`, `rights`, and `notes`. `src-I`, `src-A`, `src-X`, `src-M`, `src-U`, `src-S`, and `src-B` refer to the seven exact local files and hashes in [the rules index](../rules-index.md). PDF pages and evidence pages are **one-based PDF pages**. A file hash identifies bytes, not correctness of every face. Web revisions are observation dates unless an immutable commit is given. Community identity discovery, quantity, printed face semantics, module applicability, and official rulings are distinct claims. `rights` is an audit finding about permission evidence; it grants no reuse. No source record implies complete inventory or automated import suitability.

## Inventory expectations

An expectation contains `id`, `product`, `edition`, `category`, nullable `tier`, `expectedCopies`, nullable `uniqueDefinitions`, `unit`, `basis`, `scope`, at least one `evidence` locator (`sourceId`, one-based `page`, `locator`), and nullable `note`. Product values are exactly `Imperium`, `Uprising`, `RiseOfIx`, `Immortality`, and `Bloodlines`. Categories use current `ContentCategory` names in `ContentDefinition.cs`; `GameplayComponent` is an **audit-only** category for pieces/tokens outside the runtime catalog enum. It does not register an engine definition. `expectedCopies` is a nonnegative official quantity for the stated basis and scope. `uniqueDefinitions` is `null` when a component total gives no unique-face total; never fill it from a community row count. Known distinct starter identities, three Reserve identities, eight physical Leader cards, and 22 printed board locations are stated only where the source establishes them.

`scope` separates boxed pools, boxed tier/identity subsets, ordinary per-player recipes, standard setup components, and excluded solo/two-player, Epic, six-player, CHOAM, Tech, or leader-specific material. A subset **overlaps** its parent boxed pool and must not be summed into it. The 18 original Conflicts split 4 + 10 + 4, while ordinary setup selects 1 + 5 + 4 = 10; the latter is not the box total. Original Reserve cards split 8 + 10 + 6 = 24. Original starter copies are four ten-card player sets = 40; each set has seven identities and 2 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 10. Three-player games use only 30 starter copies. The original board's 22 locations are named spaces, not loose cards. Alternative wood/plastic Bloodlines Commanders form a seven-unit usable pool, not fourteen usable units. Uprising likewise has four boxed ten-card starter sets and a distinct ten-card per-player recipe (U p.3). Ix has four boxed Control the Spice cards but only one per player in excluded Epic mode (X p.2); Immortality has eight boxed Experimentation cards but replaces two starters per player (M pp.3,5).

`per-player-pieces` counts colored physical player pieces (Agents, cubes, discs, markers), separately from boxed bank tokens. Original I p.3 lists four large plus 20 small Solari tokens and three large plus 20 small spice tokens: those are 24 and 23 **physical pieces**, regardless of printed resource values. The agent, cube, and disc subset expectations overlap their respective per-player umbrella totals: 2 initial Agents + 1 Swordmaster = 3; 4 Influence cubes + 12 troop cubes = 16; 1 Score marker + 1 Councilor disc = 2. Do not sum a subset into its umbrella, or infer a four-color boxed piece total merely from a per-player recipe. The unlimited abstract resource bank is separate from these finite printed tokens.

Do not turn a count into a face, effect, eligibility predicate, ability, or readiness claim. Module-scoped totals remain physically boxed totals even when the selected configuration excludes them. Keep unclear edition effects, print differences, and rights disputes as explicit limitations. A future complete pool requires identity-level, quantity-level, applicability-level, and face-level evidence separately.

## Updating restricted local evidence

Acquire source files personally from the publisher or a legitimately held copy. Keep PDFs, scans, extracted text, images, spreadsheets, and scraped data in ignored local storage; do not commit them or fetched website bodies. Record the exact edition/URL or owner-held provenance, retrieval date, SHA-256 (`sha256sum <file>`), page bounds (`pdfinfo <file>`), and one-based page/section for each claim. Compare the new hash with the pinned source record. Inspect relevant page renders for symbols, tiers, layout and obscured faces; text extraction is only a search aid. Manually enter minimal, independently reviewed metadata and cite the claim. Check official FAQ/errata and module exclusions, note disagreements without choosing a convenient count, and review reuse rights before copying any text or art. Validation and generated reports must run offline, and missing optional local copyrighted files must be reported as unavailable evidence without fetching them automatically.

## S2 typed authoring contract

The validator lives in `scripts/content_coverage_model.py`; `scripts/content_coverage.py validate` checks the authored inventory offline. The seven root arrays remain `sources`, `expectations`, `content`, `capabilities`, `interactions`, `batches`, and `configurations`. Abilities are nested in their owning `content` record. All record keys and enum values are exact and case-sensitive; unknown keys, missing required keys, booleans in integer fields, and unknown products/categories/slots fail loading. Nullable quantities distinguish unknown from zero. Empty collections and incomplete faces are never complete coverage.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Source | `kind`, `title`, `locator`, `revision`, `sha256`, `pages`, `products`, `claimTypes`, `completeness`, `verification`, `rights`, `notes`, `discrepancies` | `localPath: null`, `reusePermission: false` |
| Expectation | `product`, `edition`, `category`, `tier`, `expectedCopies`, `uniqueDefinitions`, `unit`, `basis`, `scope`, `evidence`, `note` | none |
| Content | `name`, `product`, `edition`, `category`, `version`, `variantOf`, `authenticity`, `boxedQuantity`, `perPlayerQuantity`, `expectationIds`, `evidence`, `discovery`, `sourcing`, `verification`, `faceComplete`, `slots`, `abilities`, `availability` | `faceEvidence: []`, `runtimeDefinition: false`, `replaces: []`, `excludes: []`, `implementationReferences: []`, `abilityApplicability: "executable"`, `abilityApplicabilityEvidence: []` |
| Slot | `slot`, `discovery` | `reason: null`, `evidence: []`, `abilityIds: []` |

`availability` is a required typed object with `baseGames`, `requiredExpansions`, `excludedExpansions`, `requiredModules`, `excludedModules`, `modes`, `minimumSeats`, and `maximumSeats`. Base values are `Imperium`/`Uprising`; expansions are `RiseOfIx`/`Immortality`/`Bloodlines`; modules are `UprisingChoam`/`BloodlinesTech`; modes are `Standard`/`Epic`. Empty allowlists mean any. Seat bounds must be ordered within 1–6, and required/excluded sets cannot conflict. The configuration's `mode` and `modules` use these same enums. The audit composition gate enforces the runtime's direct dependencies: `BloodlinesTech` requires Bloodlines and Uprising, `UprisingChoam` requires Uprising, and Epic requires Rise of Ix with original Imperium. It does not replace the full runtime compatibility check. A selected configuration must include every applicable, reachable definition linked to its expectations, and cannot select an unavailable one. Solo/two-player, six-player, Epic, CHOAM, Tech, leader-specific, and original-versus-Uprising scopes are evaluated for the configuration before readiness; excluded scopes do not impose permanent gaps on ordinary 3/4-seat games.

Evidence claims use `sourceId`, nullable `page`, and `locator`, with optional `kind` and `verification`. Local PDF claims need a one-based page within the pinned source bound. A verified discovered content identity needs an independently verified official `identity` claim; verified content copies need an independently verified official `quantity` claim. Verified ability semantics require a `semantics` claim. `faceComplete: true` additionally needs `faceEvidence` containing a verified official whole-face `semantics` claim and a complete category-specific slot account. The required baseline is Agent/Reveal/Acquire for a card, the applicable Intrigue timing classes, PostWin/Triggered/PersistentModifier for a Conflict, Passive/SignetRing for a Leader, and Agent for a Board Space. Additional known boxes or timing abilities are explicit slots; a baseline does not assert that every other theoretical timing is present. Every listed slot is `discovered` with an ability ID or `verified-absent` with its own verified official semantics claim for a complete face. Physical-only GameplayComponents and the BoardLayout have no ability-slot baseline and cannot claim a complete ability face. Unknown printed boxes remain explicit unknown slots with reasons. The validator's category baseline must be extended through schema review when a newly verified face shows another required box. Source PDF bytes are checked against pinned hashes if present; missing local files produce warnings without a download. `reusePermission: true` requires the explicit rights value `licensed-for-copied-artifacts`; unknown or restrictive findings grant no copying permission.

A descriptor is `{ "kind": "precondition|cost|choice|role|usage|effect", "value": "concise description" }`. A blocker is `{ "id": "...", "open": true|false, "blocking": true|false, "scope": "...", "reason": "..." }`. A test reference is `{ "path": "repo-relative path", "name": "test name", "kind": "behavior|integration|privacy-replay|inventory|initialization|full-game|rl|...", "historical": false }`. A historical or `inventory` reference cannot satisfy `tested`. Implementation references are repository-relative `path::member` strings. `authenticity` is `official` or `synthetic`; `GameplayComponent` remains audit-only and cannot assert `runtimeDefinition`.

Content-level `implementationReferences` link an audited identity to an existing registry entry without claiming a complete face or supported behavior. Each object has `registryKind`, `key`, `path`, `member`, optional `slot: null`, and optional `authored: true`. Kinds are `Catalog`, `CardRule`, `BoardRule`, `IntrigueRule`, `LeaderRule`, and `ConflictRule`. The key must equal the content ID; the repository-relative path must exist; duplicate kind/key/slot references are invalid. Omitted references expose discovered but unimplemented identities. These structured links are distinct from ability-level `implementationRefs` strings.

The independent enums are discovery (`not-discovered`, `discovered`), sourcing (`unsourced`, `partially-sourced`, `fully-sourced`), verification (`unverified`, `verified`, `disputed`), classification (`primitive`, `composite`, `special`, `unresolved`), implementation (`not-implemented`, `partially-implemented`, `implemented`, `unsupported`), and validation (`not-tested`, `tested`). Slot discovery is `undiscovered`, `unreadable`, `discovered`, or `verified-absent`; the latter requires face evidence and owns no ability. Slots are `Setup`, `Passive`, `Agent`, `Reveal`, `Acquire`, `SignetRing`, `Intrigue`, `Combat`, `PostWin`, `Endgame`, `Triggered`, and `PersistentModifier`. Timings are `setup`, `passive`, `on-placement`, `on-reveal`, `on-acquire`, `on-signet-ring`, `intrigue`, `combat`, `post-win`, `endgame`, `triggered`, and `persistent`. A nested ability ID has form `contentId#slot:key`, and its matching slot lists that ID in `abilityIds`.

`basis` and `scope` are strict enums covering the authored S1 values; adding a new physical basis or applicability scope requires a reviewed schema extension. `build_report` computes one quantity result per expectation: verified copies, sourced but unverified copies, unknown-copy record count, missing residual copies (null when any copy count is unknown), overcount, known unique total (nullable), discovered definitions, and identity/quantity completeness. Face evidence and applicable slots belong to the separate ability gate, so unreadable boxes can coexist with complete identity/quantity metadata without making gameplay ready. Known overcounts and excess definitions are integrity errors. Missing residuals and unknown totals are explicit valid gaps. Configuration readiness fails closed on missing composition evidence or inventory, omitted reachable definitions, unsupported abilities or required interactions, missing authentic initializer/full-game/RL behavior evidence, or synthetic content. `implementation=implemented` records sourced executable semantics with implemented dependencies, independently of test-reference coverage. `validation=tested` requires a current behavioral, integration, or privacy/replay test. Full configuration ability support additionally requires all three current test kinds and transitively supported capability prerequisites; missing verification cannot be relabeled as missing implementation.

## D1 readiness and dependency extensions

These additive fields version only the offline audit revision; runtime configuration, ruleset, setup, authoring, support, result and replay formats are unchanged. The additions use the existing standard-library dataclass/enum decoder and pytest/xUnit infrastructure; no dependency, generic compatibility engine or Python/engine bridge is introduced.

`abilityApplicability` is the exact enum `executable|physical-only`, defaulting to executable. A physical-only claim is valid only for an official discovered `GameplayComponent` or `BoardLayout`, with no ability slots, nested abilities or `faceComplete` claim, and a verified official `applicability` claim in `abilityApplicabilityEvidence`. The separate Board Space/card/Leader records still own any executable semantics. That explicit claim lets the ability gate treat physical inventory as having no executable face; an empty ability list or an unreadable card never receives this exemption. Referenced discovered slot IDs must exist in the owning face. A verified setup fragment, such as Baron's private two-Faction choice, can be recorded without promoting the entire Leader face.

`rulingStatus` is `resolved|partially-resolved|unresolved`; it describes only the named evidence subset and is independent of implementation and validation. `scenarios` lists future behavioral, LegalActions, payment/preflight, privacy and replay obligations. `participantIds` materializes known content/ability/capability IDs. Representative expansion families without independently discovered card identities use capability participants and precise named/source subsets in `exceptions`; they never manufacture expansion content IDs.

Batch `priority` is `high|medium|low`; `order` is the authored deterministic total order. Canonical batches use distinct positive orders and place prerequisites earlier. The qualitative ordering is: mandatory original authenticity blocker, prerequisite order, shared reuse/dependent abilities, trustworthy available evidence, then complexity/privacy/determinism/architecture risk. Source-ready work can proceed before source-blocked starter/Leader work; `priorityRationale` states the tradeoff. `sourceGates` references source records, while `evidence` provides exact pages/sections and `acceptance` states missing whole-face/identity/ruling requirements. `expectedDelta` distinguishes known measurable changes from quantities that cannot be estimated before discovery. `validationRequirements` and `risks` make each batch independently reviewable. These fields are optional for legacy/synthetic validation fixtures; authored canonical records populate them. Once batches exist, every known unsupported, unimplemented or partially implemented ability must have exactly one matching `batchId` and primary membership. Missing identities remain source-discovery tasks over the materialized known subset and official pool expectations. Capability and batch prerequisite graphs reject cycles and dangling references; an ability cannot have two primary batches.

`leaderPolicy` is now `all-official|fixed-subset`. All-official maps the canonical preset's random eligible-Leader policy and cannot specify `leaderContentIds`. A fixed subset lists exactly one distinct known Leader per seat and cannot certify an all-official policy. Inventory still reconciles the selected configuration's applicable expectations and reachable definitions; selecting a few source-complete Leaders never hides missing boxed inventory or other reachable faces. Leader-specific piece expectations use that explicit selected subset. Canonical Standard3/4-seat, +Ix3/4-seat and +Ix+Immortality3/4-seat records identify their actual `preset`; `compositionTests` links the xUnit check against `ConfigurationPresets`, `RulesetComposer` and `GameInitializer`. These tests guard runtime claims without moving runtime compatibility logic into Python.

Composition needs verified official composition evidence and valid direct module dependencies and leader policy. Inventory, ability and interaction gates remain separate. A supported interaction additionally needs an implementation member and current behavior, integration and privacy/replay references. `executionBlockers` records open source/inventory/adapter/initializer gaps and prevents initialization even if later test metadata is accidentally supplied. Original presets have a typed setup recipe but a representative catalog rejects authentic initialization; expanded presets have no executable setup adapter. Authentic initialization requires official content/configuration provenance and initialization evidence; full-game and RL gates additionally require their own current evidence. RL needs **both** an RL reference and privacy/replay evidence. Synthetic terminal evidence never certifies authentic initialization, games or RL. No canonical configuration currently reaches those gates.
