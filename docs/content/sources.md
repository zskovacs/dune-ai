## Publisher documents and current rulings

The [publisher resources page](https://www.direwolfdigital.com/dune-imperium/resources/) links the original rules/player aid, Ix, Immortality, Uprising, supplements, Bloodlines, and a FAQ. All seven supplied local PDFs were rehashed and reviewed. Original [I p.3](../rules/DUNE_IMPERIUM_Rules_2020_10_26.pdf#page=3) visibly gives 67 Imperium, 40 Intrigue, 18 Conflict (4/10/4), 24 Reserve (8/10/6), eight Leaders, 31 solo/two-player Hagal and four Baron-specific tokens. The four ten-card ordinary starter sets are four copies of the seven-identity recipe (2+2+1+2+1+1+1). [I pp.4–5](../rules/DUNE_IMPERIUM_Rules_2020_10_26.pdf#page=4) set a selected ten-card Conflict deck (1/5/4), one shuffled starter deck per participant and three Reserve stacks. [A p.2](../rules/DUNE_IMPERIUM_Player_Aid_Icons_and_Board_Spaces.pdf#page=2) and I pp.17–18 list 22 original board locations. Rendered pages I 3–4, A 2 and B 2 were inspected for layout-dependent tier, icon, and component claims; no obscured face is claimed verified from those illustrations.

Expansion components and exclusions were checked at [X p.2](../rules/DUNE_IMPERIUM_RISE_OF_IX_Rulebook_22-2-11.pdf#page=2), [M pp.3–5](../rules/DUNE_IMPERIUM_IMMORTALITY_Rulebook.pdf#page=3), [U pp.3,16,18](../rules/DUNE_IMPERIUM_UPRISING_Main_Rulebook_23-10-12.pdf#page=3), [S pp.3,7](../rules/DUNE_IMPERIUM_UPRISING_Rules_Supplements_23-10-12.pdf#page=3), and [B pp.2–3,6,8](../rules/DUNE_IMPERIUM_BLOODLINES_Rulebook.pdf#page=2). Expansion totals describe boxed components; CHOAM, Tech, original-base, solo and six-player restrictions are separate. Bloodlines has 14 physical Commander Skills; its seven wood and seven plastic Commander pieces are alternate representations of seven usable units (B pp.2–3). The 12 Twisted Intrigues and ten Navigation cards are leader-specific, separate from ordinary deck pools (B p.2).

The resources page currently links [FAQ last updated January 13, 2025](https://d19y2ttatozxjp.cloudfront.net/pdfs/DUNE_IMPERIUM_FAQ_25-1-13.pdf) (four pages; retrieved SHA-256 `7b54c283357244e5107d1d0f4e87817d39297e914c2014239acbb2c460c0c6b9`). It includes first-print corrections (for example Missionaria Protectiva affiliation and two-copy statement, Ix once-per-round wording) and rulings across products. Treat it as a separate later ruling source, not proof that a supplied earlier PDF or a scanned card face contains the corrected text. Recheck its applicable entry when authoring a face. The publisher page is not a complete machine-readable card catalog. Public downloads establish reference access, not permission to redistribute PDFs or full card text/art. The [publisher fan art policy](https://www.direwolfdigital.com/fan-art-policy/) permits some community discussion content but restricts distribution of proprietary art/assets; it does not explicitly grant a bulk card dataset license.

## Acquisition and decision

Do not commit the local PDFs, fetched website bodies, spreadsheets, scans, card art, or bulk effect prose. A reviewer can personally acquire a publisher PDF or inspect a lawfully held physical copy, keep raw material in ignored local storage, record edition/URL, date, SHA-256, page bounds and one-based locator, and compare it with the pinned source. Render pages when tiers/icons/boxes depend on layout, manually map each independently verified identity and quantity, then check the latest applicable FAQ and module exclusions. Use secondary leads only to identify questions for this independent review; reject contradictory informal effect prose and treat inaccessible files as unavailable evidence. Run the audit validator offline. Request a separate rights decision before importing third-party datasets, full printed text, or images. No completeness or automated-authoring claim is made from the sources presently assessed.
