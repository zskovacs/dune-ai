# Content source assessment

Assessed 2026-10-24 for SLICE-001. The seven PDFs in [`docs/rules/`](../rules/) are the primary authority for rules and
component/setup counts. Third-party inventories are discovery aids only. Their records do not establish that a component
is available in a particular ruleset, that its effect is correct, or that Dune AI may redistribute its text or art.

## Cross-checks and discrepancies

- The [Bloodlines rulebook, PDF/printed p. 2](../rules/DUNE_IMPERIUM_BLOODLINES_Rulebook.pdf#page=2) lists **14
  Sardaukar Commander Skills**. DuneBlend's six `sardaukar` rows have `count: 2` each, totaling
  **12**. [Dune Cards Hub](https://dunecardshub.com/) displays seven Skill entries, including “Hardy (x2).” This is
  evidence of a missing DuneBlend row; a verified card-level transcription is still required before adding that
  definition or its effect.
- The [Bloodlines rulebook, PDF/printed p. 2](../rules/DUNE_IMPERIUM_BLOODLINES_Rulebook.pdf#page=2) identifies five
  Imperium cards used only with the **CHOAM Module** and two used only with the **Tech Module**. DuneBlend's Bloodlines
  Imperium records use broad values such as `compatibility: "Uprising"` or `"All"`; its JSON has no separate CHOAM/Tech
  module availability predicate. Therefore a filter on `card_set` plus `compatibility` cannot reproduce official
  component selection. The [Bloodlines setup, p. 3](../rules/DUNE_IMPERIUM_BLOODLINES_Rulebook.pdf#page=3) also excludes
  additional cards and leaders when played with original Imperium.
- The [Bloodlines Tech Module, pp. 6–7](../rules/DUNE_IMPERIUM_BLOODLINES_Rulebook.pdf#page=6) changes which Ix Tech
  tiles and board are used depending on whether all of Rise of Ix or only its Tech tiles are present. DuneBlend's
  textual `compatibility` field is not an executable statement of those conditional rules; the composer must use
  independently sourced requirements and exclusions.
- DuneBlend's workbook spells a starter field `Reveal Persuation`, carried into JSON as `reveal_persuation`; this is a
  source-schema typo. `selected` and house-blend counts describe an editor's mutable selections, not game content.
  Ratings/votes are community opinion. None should enter a canonical fingerprint or effect model without a deliberate
  schema decision.
- Dune Cards Hub includes Conspiracy and promo entries while SLICE-001's product set excludes them. DuneBlend includes
  community blend selections, not official presets. Neither site's inventory can stand in for the official setup rules.

## Import decision

No third-party catalog or images are imported in SLICE-001. For a later catalog slice, seek written permission for the
exact data and distribution use, pin a source revision, map each row to a new stable **product + category +
component/variant** identity, verify quantity and availability against the supplied rulebooks and physical cards, and
record per-definition evidence/status. Compare a generated inventory with the official component counts and applicable
module exclusions before treating it as complete. Keep effect prose out of executable rules until behavior has been
independently specified and tested. A copyright review is needed before distributing copied card text or art.
