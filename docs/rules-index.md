# Local rules index

This is a research index for SLICE-001, not a claim that these mechanics are
implemented. All seven supplied PDFs were text-extracted and read. Setup diagrams
for Ix, Immortality, Bloodlines Tech, and six-player setup were also rendered and
inspected. Extracted text and rendered images are temporary research files and are
not redistributed. A rulebook is authoritative for its own product; its explicit
combination instructions narrow or replace earlier instructions.

## Setup and selection boundaries

Official ordinary setup permits choosing leaders or choosing randomly; same-House
avoidance and low-complexity first-game choices are recommendations, not hard
incompatibilities. (I pp.2,4; U pp.2,4.) Expansion leaders can join existing pools,
subject to module/base restrictions. (X p.3; U pp.4,16,18; B pp.3,6.) Original solo
Rival leaders have a separate eligibility rule; Uprising Rivals are separate
definitions. (I p.20; S p.3; B p.8.) Six-player setup selects Commanders, Allies
and teams before seating, with alternating team order; its suggested thematic
Ally assignments are recommendations. (S pp.7–8; B p.10.)

Sequential and snake leader drafts, custom bans and fixed player-order policies
are project setup policies. None of these seven PDFs defines a snake draft. Their
implementation must not be presented as an official rule. Resolve eligible public
leader definitions before agent choices; do not hand agents engine RNG state,
private dealt options belonging to others, or hidden future setup outcomes.

See [compatibility matrix](compatibility-matrix.md) for concrete combinations,
replacement scopes and uncertainties. Catalog completeness and content rights
are assessed separately in [content sources](content/sources.md).
