## Review focus

- A catalog marked Complete with missing quantities or invalid verification fails.
- Equal totals cannot disguise wrong reserve/starter identities or conflict tiers.
- A resolved definition from another config or replacement cannot run base setup.
- Nonempty hidden zones, stable instance IDs and metadata cannot leak via policies.
- Snapshot includes unused cards, full zone order, RNG counters, board and player
  temporary state; public projection cannot mutate or reach trusted state.
