# SLICE-003 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** use the repository's custom implementer/reviewer roles, task by task. Mandatory TDD sequencing is disabled.

**Goal:** Execute sourced original Imperium turn mechanics through Player Turns with honest support gates.

**Architecture:** Extend existing runtime zones/state with a typed incremental turn engine. Keep immutable effects separate from metadata, preserve privileged snapshots, and replay ordered actions from seeded setup. Incomplete content remains gated.

**Tech Stack:** Existing .NET 10, xUnit v3, BenchmarkDotNet, mise; no dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-slice-003-design.md`, plus user's detailed SLICE-003 requirements.

## Global Constraints

- Original Imperium, Standard, no expansions, competitive 3–4 players; fixed/random leaders.
- Existing setup and RNG reused; no toy-game delegation or fabricated card/leader effects.
- Explicit synthetic provenance; no fully playable or RL-ready configuration.
- Invalid/unsupported actions preserve all state. No hidden information in observations/legal actions.
- Follow local official PDFs and record rule/test citations. No mandatory TDD sequencing.

## Review Focus

- Unsupported nested effects detected before any cost, card movement, RNG consumption or history change.
- Revealing only remaining hand cards, and buying before/between/after Reveal effects.
- Deployment counts include recruits this turn plus at most two existing garrison troops.
- Physical IDs in observations must not encode hidden cards or future deck order.
- Replay binds executable support and all pending context, not just setup zones.
