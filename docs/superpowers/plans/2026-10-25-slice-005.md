## Review Focus

1. A duplicate selector can rebind after another identical card moves: Task 1 must
   reject both a stale decision ID and a same-decision selector whose instance changed.
2. Decline must remain legal when optional success is unsupported or has no target:
   Tasks 1/2 test independent shape, support and selected-branch feasibility.
3. Returned stock can fund only later work after actual return: Task 2 tests empty
   stacks, selected success children, optional speculation, earlier acquisitions and
   generated descendants without weakening suspended reservations.
4. Trashing the executing InPlay card must preserve its pending effects and owner:
   Tasks 3/4 test source/target distinction, foreign child resume and draw ordering.
5. A green synthetic run or primitive must not promote a whole face/readiness gate:
   Tasks 4/5 prove actual board behavior, leave Foldspace/Leaders/pools unsupported,
   and keep all six authentic configurations unready.
